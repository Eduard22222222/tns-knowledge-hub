# Personas — The Niche Society

## Persona 1 — The Patient (Client)
**Name:** Ioana, 28, Bucharest
**Context:** Marketing manager, disposable income, goes to a dermatologist for acne + occasional botox. Scrolls Instagram, books everything via DM.

**Pains:**
- WhatsApps her doctor, messages get lost in 200 other chats
- Forgets to apply creams, treatment takes longer than it should
- Loses paper prescriptions
- Never knows if she "graduated" the treatment
- Wants to refer her sister but no system for it

**Wants:**
- One app per clinic (not 20 generic health apps)
- Quick replies from doctor
- Visible progress (before/after, streaks)
- Perks for being loyal
- Product recommendations she can trust

**Success metric for us:** weekly active use, adherence rate >70%, NPS >50

---

## Persona 2 — The Doctor
**Name:** Dr. Andrei, 42, dermatologist, co-owner of a 3-chair clinic
**Context:** Sees 15–25 patients/day, spends evenings replying to WhatsApp. Knows he's losing patients to follow-up drop-off.

**Pains:**
- WhatsApp chaos, no record, no search
- Can't tell who's adherent vs who ghosted
- Repetitive questions ("can I shower after laser?")
- No easy way to upsell products or packages
- Admin work > medicine

**Wants:**
- Inbox, not group chat
- AI drafting replies for common questions
- Adherence dashboard
- Legitimate way to recommend + earn on products
- Less typing

**Success metric for us:** reduced response time, lower patient churn, higher avg revenue per patient

---

## Persona 3 — The Clinic Admin / Owner
**Name:** Raluca, 38, owns a 2-location aesthetic clinic
**Context:** Manages staff, cashflow, marketing, supplies. Burned on software that over-promised.

**Pains:**
- 4 different tools (booking, invoicing, CRM, social)
- Google reviews are hard to get
- Referrals happen but untracked, unrewarded
- GDPR is a looming threat, no one handles it for her
- Can't see what's working

**Wants:**
- One branded platform, her logo on it
- Real metrics (retention, LTV, referral rate)
- GDPR done-for-you
- Easy loyalty/referral that doesn't require a marketing hire
- Monthly price that pays itself back in 1–2 extra clients

**Success metric for us:** logo NPS, retention, upsell to higher tier

# The Niche Society — Vault

White-label clinic platform for Romanian dermatology, aesthetic, dental, and wellness clinics.

## 3 interfaces
1. **Client app** — conversate with doctor, track prescriptions, get product recommendations, earn rewards
2. **Doctor app** — monitor adherence, chat support, prescribe, recommend products
3. **Admin app (clinic owner)** — tenant settings, staff, pricing, referral/MLM config, reporting

## 2 core layers
- **Layer 1 — Care layer**: doctor↔patient chat, prescription adherence, AI assistance, product recs
- **Layer 2 — Growth layer**: MLM-style referral program, loyalty points, prizes (money / free treatments / points)

## Vault structure
- `01-Brainstorming` — raw ideas, concept expansion
- `02-Market-Research` — RO clinic market, patient demand
- `03-Product-Definition` — personas, features, MVP scope
- `04-Architecture` — system design, multi-tenant model
- `05-Compliance-GDPR` — medical data rules in EU/RO
- `06-Business-Model` — pricing, monetization
- `07-MLM-Referral-Layer` — referral program design, legal limits
- `08-AI-Layer` — agents, RAG, safety
- `09-Competitors` — who else is doing this
- `10-Notes-Daily` — daily logs
- `11-Branding-Social-Media` — brand TNS în online: LinkedIn (companie + profil Eduard), motor de postări, backlog conținut

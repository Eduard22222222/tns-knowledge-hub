# Intersnack Brand Showcase

**Client:** Intersnack Group (https://www.intersnack.ro/)
**Status:** Code scaffold complete — awaiting client sign-off
**Dev server:** http://localhost:8086/ (port 8086)

## Key design decision: "Idei de servire" instead of "Retete"

Since these are snack brands (not cooking ingredients), the platform uses **"Idei de servire"** (serving ideas) instead of traditional recipes. This covers party platters, dip pairings, snack mixes, movie night combos, kids' party setups, gourmet cheese board pairings, etc.

## The 3 brands

### 1. Chio (flagship salty snacks)
- **Colors:** red `#DB2327` → `#9B1B1E`
- **Tagline:** "Da crunch momentului!"
- **Products:** Chips Classic Sare, Tortilla Sour Cream, Popcorn Sare
- **Idei de servire:** Nachos loaded, dip platters, caramel popcorn, chip-crusted chicken, tortilla pizza bites

### 2. Tyrrells (premium artisan crisps)
- **Colors:** plum `#4A1942` → cream `#C9A96E`
- **Tagline:** "Hand-cooked, properly good crisps."
- **Products:** Mature Cheddar & Chive, Veg Crisps, Lentil Sweet Chilli
- **Idei de servire:** Gourmet cheese board, salad crunch topping, fish & crisps, soup topping, modern bruschetta

### 3. Pom-Bar (family/kids snacks)
- **Colors:** yellow `#FFB800` → orange `#FF8C00`
- **Tagline:** "Ursuletii crocanti pentru toata familia!"
- **Products:** Original Cascaval 40g, Smantana 50g, Family Pack Sare 65g
- **Idei de servire:** Kids party platter, after-school snack mix, picnic setup, movie night bowl, cheese dip

## Content highlights

- 9 competitions (nachos contest, movie night challenge, kids party, wine pairing, etc.)

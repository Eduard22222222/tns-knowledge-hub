# Niche Society — Dermatology

Child vault of [[The Niche Society Vault/README|The Niche Society]].
White-label SaaS template for **medical dermatology clinics** in Romania (acne, psoriasis, eczema, mole monitoring, pediatric derm, medical lasers).

## Why dermatology is separate from aesthetic
- Medical derm = insurance, prescription-heavy, chronic condition tracking
- Aesthetic = cash-pay, transformation-focused
- Different patient journey, different AI features, different compliance surface
- Shared codebase, different feature flags + flows

## Planned derm-specific features (to detail in 01-Features)
- **AI Skincare Routine Builder** — patient inputs skin type + owned products → AI drafts AM/PM routine → doctor approves
- Mole / lesion photo tracking with ABCDE scoring
- Chronic condition diaries (acne, psoriasis, eczema)
- Prescription OCR for Romanian paper prescriptions
- Pediatric mode (parent accounts)
- Severity scoring scales (PASI, EASI, IGA)

# Consumer Voice Brand Guidelines

Brandbook file: `C:/Users/adumi/OneDrive/Pictures/Folder nou/consumer voice/brandbook-consumervoice.pdf`

## Colors
- **Celestial Blue:** `#498CCA` — RGB(73, 140, 202) — trust, transparency
- **Sunglow (Yellow):** `#FEC136` — RGB(254, 193, 54) — warmth, community

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Mostra Nuova Bold
- **Subheadlines:** Arbotek Light
- Rounded shapes, consistent line weights — clarity, openness, consumer-centered

## Logo
- Logo at: `C:/Users/adumi/OneDrive/Pictures/Folder nou/consumer voice/descărcare-removebg-preview.png`
- Tagline: "real voices, real impact"
- Logo anatomy: speech bubble + text
- No skewing, stretching, or distortion
- Fixed clear space around logo

## Values
- Authentic voices, honest reviews, credible feedback
- Empowering consumer decisions
- Trust, transparency, community

## When Doing Proposals
- Use Consumer Voice branding ONLY (not TNS)
- TNS and Consumer Voice have completely different brand styles
- Blue backgrounds for cover/closing pages
- Yellow accents for highlights, buttons, dividers
- Reference: Serpico v13 (`Propunere_Serpico_v13.pdf`) for layout style

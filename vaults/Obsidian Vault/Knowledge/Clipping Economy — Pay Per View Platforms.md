# Clipping Economy — Pay Per View Platforms

The money map behind the [[Clip Factory]]. A brand/streamer funds a **pool**, publishes a brief,
and pays a **CPM** (cost per 1,000 verified views) for short clips of their content. Platform
verifies views via social APIs and pays out. Typical CPM **$0.30–$10/1K**, platform fee 7–9%,
per-clip caps common (~$1,000). Below-minimum-view clips can pay $0.

## The marketplaces
- **ContentRewards.com** — branded front door to Whop's Content Rewards (by Whop's marketing lead,
  Brett Malinowski). CPM $1–$10/1K (some $0.30 on huge pools), 7% fee. $1.7M+ paid to 98k+
  creators. Discover live campaigns at `contentrewards.com/discover`.
- **Whop Clips** — the underlying engine. Typical $0.50–$2/1K; YouTube-clip type ~$1.25/1K; some
  flat $5–50/post. 9% fee. Per-clip caps ~$1,000.
- **Vieewzz.com ("Vieews")** — **Romania-focused** pay-per-view clipping/UGC. TikTok/IG/Shorts,
  **no follower minimum, anyone joins.** Fills the gap (no TikTok Creator Fund in RO). Exact CPM
  not public — scan live briefs. **Priority for us**: local, less saturated, RO-language edge.
- **ViewPaid** ($1–5/1K), **ClipAffiliates**, **Ssemble Clip Rewards** — diversification so one
  program pausing doesn't zero the day.

## The economics that decide what to chase
`EV/clip = CPM × (expected_views/1000) × (1 − fee)`, capped at any per-clip cap. Then rank by
**EV ÷ minutes-to-produce**. Rules of thumb:
- High CPM ÷ low difficulty wins; a rich, clippable source beats a high CPM on a boring source.
- Deep pool + easy brief + generous cap beats a high CPM with a $200 cap.
- `expected_views/clip` is the number that matters most — start conservative, learn it fast from
  real posts.

## Realistic earnings (industry)
$300–800/mo casual, $1,500–3,000/mo full-time, $5,000+ top performers. Not passive — it's a volume
+ distribution game (see [[Multi-Account Clip Distribution — Anti-Shadowban]]).

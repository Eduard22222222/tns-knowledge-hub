## Organic Reach Assumptions (2026 FMCG benchmarks)
- Facebook organic reach: 2-5% of followers per post
- Click-through rate on organic posts: 0.5-1.5%
- Landing page conversion (click → signup): 15-30%
- Instagram Reels can outperform static by 3-5x in reach

## Strategic Role
- Organic social is not the volume driver — it's the amplifier
- Best use: repost UGC from in-store users → their networks see → join
- Recipe content, review highlights, competition announcements
- Supports paid campaigns — organic winners get boosted
- Content farm effect: users generate high-engagement content at zero production cost

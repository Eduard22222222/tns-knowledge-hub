# Niche Society — Dermatology Platform

White-label SaaS template for medical dermatology clinics in Romania. Second of 3 verticals under [[The Niche Society]].

## Context
- Parent initiative: [[The Niche Society]]
- Sibling verticals: [[Niche Society Aesthetic Platform]], [[Niche Society Dental Platform]]
- Started: 2026-04-17
- Phase: Vault prepped, build follows after Aesthetic template ships

## Why separate from aesthetic
- Medical derm = insurance, prescription-heavy, chronic condition tracking
- Aesthetic = cash-pay, transformation-focused
- Different patient journey, different AI features, shared codebase with feature flags

## Derm-specific features
- **AI Skincare Routine Builder** (flagship viral feature) — patient inputs skin type + owned products → AI drafts AM/PM routine → doctor approves
- Mole / lesion photo tracking with ABCDE scoring
- Chronic condition diaries (acne, psoriasis, eczema)
- Prescription OCR for RO paper prescriptions
- Pediatric mode (parent accounts)
- Severity scoring scales (PASI, EASI, IGA)

# Consumer Voice — Consumer Community Platform (the offer)

The product we sell via cold outreach. Sourced from `Downloads/Consumervoice consumer platform proposal.docx` (Wave → Kandia proposal) + consumervoice.eu + waveadvertising.ro.

## The platform — Four tools, one platform
Each tool delivers value across THREE internal audiences (this 3-audience matrix is the core differentiator — sell to Marketing AND Sales AND Trade Marketing):

| Tool | Marketing | Sales | Trade Marketing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Verified Reviews & Ratings** (star ratings + verified reviews from buyers) | Authentic social proof for ads/PDPs/content; brand-level sentiment to sharpen messaging | Proof of demand + product quality to win listings & defend shelf | Product-level review data for range reviews & joint business plans |
| **Recipe & Inspiration Hub** (branded recipes + consumer UGC) | Steady UGC + usage-occasion pipeline to repurpose | Occasion stories that justify bundles & cross-merch | Recipe-led activations & basket-building, in-store + online |
| **Purchase-Based Promotions & Contests** (receipt entry, prize draws, photo/recipe-video contests) | Reach + measurable participation w/ ready creative | Receipt data proving sell-through & repeat purchase | Retailer-specific mechanics to secure promo slots & co-funding |
| **Always-On Consumer Feedback** (continuous preference/need/trend capture) | Trend & preference data for positioning + NPD briefs | Demand signals + emerging-need evidence for account talks | Category & innovation insight for range/shopper plans |

## The pitch / goal
Help each brand build its own **proprietary consumer asset** — generates engagement, authentic advocacy, applicable consumer intelligence, and measurable business value long after individual campaigns end. Run **per brand** (each brand = dedicated community space) while insight **rolls up to a single portfolio-level view**. Strengthens loyalty, builds a direct ongoing brand↔consumer relationship, and gives the portfolio a sustainable source of **first-party data** (post-cookie advantage).

## Investment model (LEAVE OFF the landing page — Eduard's call; for reference/proposal only)
- Platform development **WAIVED** in exchange for a **12-month non-cancellable** agreement (initial 3 brands).
- **€300/brand/month** maintenance. Volume discount: 3 brands €3,600/brand/yr (€15k ACV) → 4 brands −5% → 5 brands −8% → 6+ brands −12% (€3,168/brand/yr, €27,408 ACV).
- Competition **setup €150** / activation, **monitoring €200** / activation (not discounted).
- Min **1 competition/brand/quarter**. 3–5 SKUs + 1 Consumer Voice Widget per brand.
- Included in maintenance: review aggregation, AI review analysis, KPI recommendations + watch-outs, social sharing of 4.5★+ reviews, 1–2 recipe uploads/brand, marketing insights.
- **Dev timeline: 3–5 weeks.**

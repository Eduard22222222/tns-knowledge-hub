## The Challenge (48h)

- Identifică un real-world business skill or problem (strategy, sales, negotiation, operations, etc.)
- Build un functional game / simulation prototype (web / app / platform)
- Mechanics must enable learning-by-doing prin: decision-making, feedback loops, consequences & outcomes, progression systems

## Milestones (toate 4 contează)

1. **Identify** un business problem și validate it
2. **Build** un functional MVP folosind vibe-coding platforms
3. **Acquire** at least 100 users care joacă jocul
4. **Monetize** — generate $100 revenue făcând părți din joc paid

## Judging criteria

1. Problem Relevance & Clarity
2. Product Thinking & UX
3. Quality of Simulation & Learning Experience
4. Creativity in Game Design
5. Impact on User Understanding of Business

## Lead concept: Pitch Pit (AI Shark-Tank)

Un AI Shark-Tank — pitchezi business idea-ul tău unui AI judge (Claude API), AI te interogheazã contra-argumentând ca un VC, primești scor + feedback + roast la final care e shareable. Loop natural pentru viralizare (1 user → roast share → 5 users).

Vezi vault sursă pentru detalii Concept-Locked-CloseIt și V2-LifeGame.

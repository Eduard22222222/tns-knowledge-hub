## Macromex Projection Model

### Assumptions
- 50+ stores per weekend campaign (minimum 40)
- 2 retail chains per campaign
- 800 registrations/reviews per chain = 1,600 per weekend campaign
- 3 campaigns per year
- 15% incremental growth per campaign (brand awareness, word of mouth, better execution)

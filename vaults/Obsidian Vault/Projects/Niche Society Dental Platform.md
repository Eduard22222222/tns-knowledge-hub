# Niche Society — Dental Platform

White-label SaaS template for dental clinics in Romania. Third of 3 verticals under [[The Niche Society]].

## Context
- Parent initiative: [[The Niche Society]]
- Sibling verticals: [[Niche Society Aesthetic Platform]], [[Niche Society Dermatology Platform]]
- Started: 2026-04-17
- Phase: Vault prepped, build follows Aesthetic + Dermatology

## Why dental is its own template
- Treatment plans span months/years (orthodontics, implants)
- Visual tooth chart is THE central UI
- Financing / payment plans common (€1k–€15k tickets)
- X-ray / CBCT viewer required
- Family accounts common

## Dental-specific features
- **Interactive tooth chart** (FDI 11–48) — clickable, color-coded status
- Treatment plan timeline with cost breakdown
- X-ray / photo viewer with annotations
- Financing calculator (monthly plan vs lump sum)
- Orthodontic progress tracker (aligners, bracket photos)
- Family account linking
- Recall reminders (6-month cleaning)

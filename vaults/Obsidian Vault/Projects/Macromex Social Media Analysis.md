## Key Observations
1. **Corso dominates** with 135K FB likes — strongest brand equity
2. **La Strada** has solid 65K FB — fashion/premium positioning
3. **Edenia** has 40K FB but strongest Instagram game (7.8K)
4. **Instagram is underdeveloped across all brands** relative to Facebook
5. **No TikTok presence found** for any brand — massive gap/opportunity
6. Facebook pages are legacy audiences — engagement likely low (typical for FMCG pages this age)

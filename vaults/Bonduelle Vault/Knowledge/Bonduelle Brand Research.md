# Bonduelle România — Brand Research

## Company snapshot

Bonduelle is a French family-owned agri-food company, founded in 1853. Romania operation is one of their core CEE markets. Positioning: fresh, natural, health-conscious vegetable products — both canned and frozen.

## Product portfolio

### Categories

- **Legume în conservă** — canned vegetables (flagship)
- **Legume congelate** — frozen vegetables

### Key product lines (from bonduelle.ro/produse)

- Porumb dulce (sweet corn) — the iconic Bonduelle SKU
- Mazăre fină (fine peas)
- Fasole roșie / albă / verde (red/white/green beans)
- Ciuperci (mushrooms, sliced)
- Broccoli
- Amestec de legume / Mix de legume
- Castravete, Conopidă, Spanac

## Content strategy (from bonduelle.ro/retete)

Their website leans heavily on **recipes**. The `/retete` section is a full content hub with Romanian-language recipes using their products. This is the strongest angle for the consumer platform we're building.

Example recipes featured:

- Paste cu ciuperci feliate și legume în sos de roșii
- Orez cu ciuperci feliate și legume în sos de roșii
- Tocăniță cu năut, ciuperci feliate și legume
- Salată cu portocale, varză roșie și Crochete Croustis

## Visual identity

- **Primary color:** green `#00843D` (Bonduelle corporate green)
- **Logo:** `bonduelle-logo.png` — white leaf emblem on green
- **Tone:** fresh, natural, French heritage + Romanian accessibility
- **Imagery:** close-up food photography, natural light, rustic plating

## Opportunity for TNS

1. **Consumer review loop** — they don't currently have a strong user-generated review system. Our platform adds this.
2. **Recipe engagement hub** — amplify their existing recipes with user photos, ratings, variations.
3. **Competition engine** — monthly giveaways tied to product purchases drive repeat buys and reviews.
4. **First-party data** — move review collection off third-party platforms onto their own domain.

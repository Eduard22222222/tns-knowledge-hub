# Scandia Food — Brand Research

## Company snapshot

Scandia Food is a Romanian food manufacturer with **over 100 years** of history, headquartered in Sibiu. Market leader in the canned meat products category in Romania, with a major presence in vegetable, meat, and prepared meal conserves. In the last 15 years they've aggressively diversified into:

- Mezeluri (cold cuts)
- Conserve cu pește (fish — Tapas, Lotka, Năvodul Plin)
- Produse congelate (frozen foods)
- Legume la conservă (canned vegetables — Rotina, Bălea)
- Sucuri naturale (Vitalique)
- Jeleuri, ulei de măsline, hrană pentru animale

Corporate tagline: **"Viață plină de savoare!"** (Life full of flavor!)

## The 5 brands in the portfolio

### Scandia Sibiu (flagship heritage)
- **Category:** Ready meals + premium pate + canned meats
- **Heritage:** Named for the Sibiu origin city. Full product line-up with slogan variant "SCANDIA SIBIU logo slogan.png"
- **Products observed on shop:** Ciorbă de perisoare 400g, Ciorbă de burtă 400g, Pate de fazan 120g, Pate de curcan 200g, Pate de pui integral 200g, Carne de vită în suc propriu 300g
- **Web page:** https://scandia.ro/scandia-sibiu/

### Bucegi (pate ficat icon)
- **Category:** Exclusively pate ficat (liver pate)
- **Heritage:** Romania's most recognized pate brand, "de peste 50 de ani"
- **Products observed:** Pate porc 300g/200g/120g, Pate pui 300g/200g/120g, Pastă pentru ouă umplute 200g (two variants — plain and with gogoșari), Pate cu ficat de gâscă 120g, Pate porc cu ardei/ciuperci/unt 120g, Pate vegetal (yes, they make vegetarian pate too)
- **Web page:** https://scandia.ro/bucegi/

### Sadu (hearty canned meat)
- **Category:** Canned meat (carne de vită, porc, pasăre, Luncheon Meat)
- **Heritage:** Named for the Sadu river in the Sibiu region
- **Products observed:** Carne presată pasăre 300g, Carne de vită 300g, Luncheon Meat 300g, Carne de porc 300g
- **Web page:** https://scandia.ro/sadu/

### Rotina (parked for v2)
- **Category:** Canned vegetables (Mazăre, Fasole, Linte, Năut, etc.)
- **Web page:** https://scandia.ro/rotina/

### Bălea (parked for v2)
- **Category:** Canned vegetables variant, named for the Bălea Lake
- **Web page:** https://scandia.ro/conservebalea/

## Visual identity

- **Corporate logo:** `scandia-food9123.svg` — clean monochrome wordmark
- **Corporate palette:** based on the Facebook cover photo, leans earth tones and warm reds/burgundies befitting a heritage food brand
- **Our chosen palettes per brand (designer picks):**
  - Scandia Sibiu: burgundy `#6B1A1D` + gold `#A8751A` — heritage, warmth
  - Bucegi: forest green `#2D4A1F` + olive `#6B8040` — natural, rustic
  - Sadu: deep red `#8B1E1E` + charcoal `#3A2A2A` — strong, reliable

## Content strategy observations

- **Shop-first:** They run `shop.scandia.ro` on Gomag, with banners for seasonal campaigns (Paste 2026, Campania Roua martie, Năvodul Plin Februarie, Lotka Ton, Bucegi Pate)
- **Seasonal campaigns:** Heavy focus on Easter (ouă umplute), summer grilling, winter comfort food
- **No user reviews:** scandia.ro does not currently have a consumer review system — clear opportunity for our platform
- **No recipe hub:** Shop has products but no recipe content — another opportunity

## Opportunity for TNS

1. **Consumer review loop** — they have zero first-party reviews. Our platform fills this gap brand-by-brand.
2. **Recipe hub** — showcase authentic Romanian dishes made with Scandia products, drives purchase intent.
3. **Seasonal competitions** — Easter, Summer BBQ, Winter comfort food cycles are ready-made activation moments.
4. **Brand storytelling** — 100+ year heritage is currently underused in their digital marketing.
5. **First-party data** — move engagement off social and onto their owned platform.

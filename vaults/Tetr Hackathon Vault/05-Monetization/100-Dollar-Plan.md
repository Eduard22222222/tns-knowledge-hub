# $100 Revenue Plan

Math: $100 / $7 Pro = **15 sales**. Out of 100 free players, 15 is a 15% paid conversion. Aggressive but plausible with the right paywall triggers. Realistic baseline: 3-5% → ~400 users needed for $100 via $7 Pro. We'll price more aggressively to compress that.

## Pricing ladder

| Tier | Price | Unlocks |
|------|-------|---------|
| Free | $0 | 2 pitches/day, 4 base sharks |
| **Pro (one-time)** | **$9** | Unlimited pitches, 8 sharks incl. Zuck/Jobs/Sara Blakely, post-mortem PDF, scorecard export in HD |
| Pro+ | $29 | Everything in Pro + "Custom Shark" (paste a real VC's public profile, we clone their voice) + "Deck Generator" (your pitch → 10-slide deck PDF) |

Why **one-time** not subscription: in a 48h window, friction matters more than LTV. One-time purchases close faster. Subscription is a v2 discussion after we win.

## Paywall triggers (the only 3 that matter)
1. **Immediately after a great score** — "3/4 sharks invested. Unlock the other 4 to see if you survive the A-team." (emotion + FOMO)
2. **After hitting the daily limit** — "Come back tomorrow, OR unlock unlimited for $9" (convenience)
3. **On the share card** — Pro badge is visible on shared scorecards. Driving "how did they get that?" → organic paid funnel.

## The $100 Target — 3 scenarios

**Scenario A: Low viral (60 users total)**  
- 60 users, 8% paid = ~5 sales @ $9 = **$45**. Miss.
- Mitigation: run a $1 "first-pitch upgrade" trial for the launch day, converts a spikier %. Or sell 1 Pro+ at $29 to hit $74. Either way, borderline.

**Scenario B: Normal viral (150 users total)**  
- 150 × 6% = 9 sales × $9 = **$81**. Borderline. Add 1 Pro+ at $29 → **$110**. ✅
- Realistic if we hit 100 users by H42 per the playbook.

**Scenario C: Above-target (400+ users)**  
- 400 × 5% = 20 sales × $9 = **$180**+. Easy ✅.

## Tactical $ boosters
- **Launch-day "Founder's Discount"** countdown on the pricing page — $9 → $6 for 24h. Creates urgency even if $9 was never the "real" price.
- **Pay-with-a-tweet option** for users with 1000+ followers in lieu of Pro — we lose the $9 but gain distribution. Only enable if organic virality is weak.
- **Reach out directly** to 5 people we know will buy it just to support us — ethical, fastest $45. Count them separately in the hackathon submission for transparency.
- **Bundle** a "Pitch Pit Pro + custom pitch feedback from a real founder" pack at $49 if we have a mentor willing to do 30 min. 2 of these = $98.

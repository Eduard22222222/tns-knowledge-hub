# Step 2 — Environments

Every scenario gets a distinct, atmospheric environment. Consistent art style across all: **low-poly isometric 3D-render look** (static images generated with Nanobanana/Higgsfield, no real 3D engine). Warm color grading, soft rim light, deliberate "diorama" framing.

## Why this style wins

1. **Feasible in 48h** — static renders, no engine, no rigging, no animation pipeline
2. **Screenshots sell the game** — looks like a premium indie game, not a chatbot
3. **Consistent "world"** — same art direction across 8 scenes builds brand
4. **Scales to video** — TikTok demos of the game will look cinematic

## Universal rules (apply to every scene)

- **Framing**: ~35° isometric camera, slight top-down
- **Lighting**: warm key light, cool fill, soft shadows
- **Palette**: each scene has a dominant "mood hue" (see per-scene table) + shared accent gold
- **Detail density**: LOW — one hero prop, 3 supporting props, nothing busy
- **No text in the render itself** — all text is HTML/CSS overlay (so it's localizable + editable)
- **Character presence**: counterpart portrait in bottom-left corner as a 2D card (with mouth/expression swap for Sims-style speaking), environment fills behind

## The Hub — "Your Desk"

Before you enter any scenario, you're at **your desk**. Low-poly isometric render: a cozy home office at golden hour. Laptop open. Coffee mug. Plant. Window with city skyline. Phone on the desk — when it rings, a new scenario is available. A small clipboard to the right with your "progress" (XP bar, tactics unlocked, stars-earned map).

This is the **neutral space** the player always returns to. Simple, warm, no pressure.

**Mira** — your coach character — lives as a small animated portrait card in the corner of the desk ("coffee cup icon"). Click her between scenarios to replay Lesson Cards, or she pops in automatically after a scene to deliver the fresh lesson.

## The 8 Scene Environments

### 1. 📞 Cold Call — "The Gym Floor"
**What you see**: Low-poly gym interior. Dumbbells racked on one wall, kettlebells on a rubber-floor platform, a squat rack with weights mid-set, a whiteboard with the day's workout, one silhouetted client stretching in the back. Morning sun through a high window catches dust in the air.
**POV twist**: You're NOT in the gym — you're on the phone. So Marcus is mid-walk through this gym, phone to ear. The isometric frame shows the gym + his portrait in the bottom-left. A small **phone-call UI overlay** (call timer ticking, mute button, "On Call with Marcus" label) sits at the top — a subtle reminder this is voice-only.
**Mood hue**: warm oranges + wood browns + black rubber + white walls
**Ambience**: plates clinking, hip-hop low in background, distant grunt, occasional squeak of sneakers
**Prompt seed for art**: *"Isometric low-poly 3D render of a small neighborhood gym interior, morning sunlight streaming through high windows catching dust, dumbbells racked on left wall, squat rack center, rubber flooring, whiteboard with workout written in chalk, one silhouetted person stretching in background, warm orange and wood tones, diorama style, no characters in foreground."*

### 2. 💼 Interview — "The Glass Conference Room"
**What you see**: Modern corporate meeting room. Glass wall overlooking an open-plan office beyond. Two chairs at a small table. Laptop open, screen visible but angled away. Water pitcher + two glasses. One succulent in a concrete pot.
**POV**: Slight overhead angle, showing Dan across the table (his card bottom-left), empty chair in the foreground (that's you).
**Mood hue**: cool blues + white + light wood accents
**Ambience**: HVAC hum, very distant keyboard clicks, a muffled phone ring from outside the room
**Prompt seed**: *"Isometric low-poly 3D render of a modern corporate glass-walled meeting room, small round table with two chairs, laptop on table, water pitcher, succulent in concrete pot, cool daylight, open-office blurred in background through glass, minimalist Scandinavian design, cream and navy palette, diorama style, no characters."*

### 3. 💰 Salary Negotiation — "The HR Corner Office"
**What you see**: Diana's actual office, not a sterile room. Warmer. Plants on the windowsill. A framed photo of her Australian Shepherd on the desk. A coffee mug that says "Mondays." Late afternoon golden light through venetian blinds casting stripes.
**POV**: Close, almost intimate — you're across a desk from her, she has your file open in front of her.
**Mood hue**: warm terracotta + sage green + cream + brass fixtures
**Ambience**: quieter, more intimate — a clock ticks, occasional paper rustle, elevator ping in the distance
**Prompt seed**: *"Isometric low-poly 3D render of a warm HR director's corner office at golden hour, wooden desk with open folder and coffee mug, desk plant, framed dog photo, venetian blinds casting warm light stripes, cozy but professional, terracotta and sage palette, diorama style, no characters."*

### 4. 🎯 Marketing Pricing — "The Zoom Split"
**What you see**: Stylized Zoom UI as the centerpiece — two video tiles side by side. Your tile shows your low-poly home office (plant, second monitor visible). Lauren's tile shows a WeWork-style booth, sticker-covered laptop. Each tile's background is a rendered environment, not a real photo.
**POV**: Locked on the grid view.
**Mood hue**: cool white neutral with teal + warm accent per tile
**Ambience**: soft Zoom connection chime at start, muted typing, camera-mic pop when she unmutes
**Prompt seed**: *"Isometric low-poly 3D render of a split Zoom-call view with two environment tiles: left tile a cozy home office with desk plant and dual monitors in warm light, right tile a modern co-working booth with sticker-covered laptop in cool light, stylized video-call UI frame around both, minimalist diorama style, no characters."*

### 5. 🏢 Enterprise Discovery — "The Dark Boardroom (Camera Off)"
**What you see**: Your Zoom tile is visible + Alex's tile shows just a company logo on a dark gray background (he's camera off — that's the whole power move). Behind the Zoom UI, ghost-rendered in low opacity, is a sense of his boardroom: dark wood table, city skyline through floor-to-ceiling glass, amber lamps.
**POV**: Zoom UI foreground; ambient boardroom as atmospheric layer behind.
**Mood hue**: charcoal + deep navy + amber highlights
**Ambience**: very quiet, distant PA system, clicking of his pen
**Prompt seed**: *"Isometric low-poly 3D render of a dark corporate boardroom at dusk, long dark wood conference table, black leather chairs, floor-to-ceiling glass wall showing city skyline with amber lights, minimal ambient lighting from a single overhead lamp, moody and imposing, diorama style, no characters."*

### 6. 🎭 Sales Coach — "The London Study"
**What you see**: Paul's wood-paneled home study, the kind that says "I've already succeeded." Floor-to-ceiling bookshelf with sales books and a globe. Leather Chesterfield armchair. Unlit fireplace with candles on the mantle. His dog — a retriever — sleeping on a Persian rug. Rain streaking the window.
**POV**: Slight cinematic angle, Paul's webcam framed on his desk so we see the full room.
**Mood hue**: deep oak brown + forest green + cream + brass
**Ambience**: antique clock ticking, rain on the window, occasional low fire crackle (even unlit — sound library permission), dog sigh
**Prompt seed**: *"Isometric low-poly 3D render of an upscale London home study, wood-paneled walls, floor-to-ceiling bookshelf with globe and decorative books, leather Chesterfield armchair, fireplace with candles on mantle, Persian rug, golden retriever sleeping on rug, rain-streaked window, warm moody lighting, scholarly and expensive, diorama style, no characters."*

### 7. 🎤 Pitch Q&A — "The Spotlit Stage"
**What you see**: Competition stage viewed slightly from the side. Wooden podium center stage with your laptop + water bottle. Long judges' table 3m in front. 3 chairs, 3 microphones, 3 water glasses. Projector screen behind you showing your final slide (title card of your startup, name TBD). Audience in shadow beyond the judges, just silhouettes of ~80 people. Dramatic stage lighting.
**POV**: Three-quarter stage view — the room is the character. When a judge speaks, a spotlight animation highlights them + their portrait card slides up.
**Mood hue**: high contrast — dark audience + hot stage key light + screen glow blue
**Ambience**: hush of attention, occasional cough, HVAC, the faint echo of the large room
**Prompt seed**: *"Isometric low-poly 3D render of a small startup pitch competition stage, wooden podium center with laptop, long judges table with 3 chairs and microphones in front, projector screen behind showing blank pitch slide, dark audience silhouettes in background, dramatic theatrical spotlighting, blue-amber contrast, diorama style, no characters on stage."*

### 8. 🚨 Press Crisis — "The Car"
**What you see**: First-person-ish driver's seat view, slightly offset to isometric. Your hands on the wheel. Phone on dash mount, call-connected screen showing *"Nathan Walsh, TechCrunch Europe · 00:03"*. AirPods icon. Traffic light glowing red ahead. City morning — overcast, grey, drizzle. Coffee cup in the holder, half empty.
**POV**: Inside the car, dashboard + windshield + the phone as hero prop.
**Mood hue**: moody grey + cool blue + warm car-interior amber + red traffic light accent
**Ambience**: turn signal ticking, windshield wipers low, traffic hum, your own breathing on the AirPods
**Prompt seed**: *"Isometric low-poly 3D render of a car interior from driver's seat perspective, dashboard with mounted smartphone showing active call, hands on steering wheel, AirPods case on dashboard, coffee cup in holder, rainy overcast city visible through windshield, red traffic light ahead, moody grey and amber palette, cinematic, diorama style."*

## Character portraits (bottom-left card)

For each counterpart, we generate **3 expression variants**:
- Neutral (default)
- Pleased / leaning-in (when rapport is rising)
- Annoyed / closed-off (when rapport drops)

All portraits: same low-poly 3D style, plain soft-color background (matches their scene's mood hue), head and shoulders, consistent cel-shaded lighting.

**Mouth animation**: 2-frame swap (closed / open) cycled at ~5Hz while the simlish audio loop plays and the dialogue text types. Creates the Sims-speaking effect with zero animation work.

**Portraits needed for MVP**: 8 counterparts × 3 expressions = 24 portraits. Plus Mira (coach) × 3 = 27 total.

Gen budget: ~30 minutes on Nanobanana (with one reference image to lock style).

---

## Transitions between scenes and hub

1. **Desk (hub)** → click phone on desk → camera zooms INTO the phone screen → fade → **Scene**
2. **End of scene** → fade out → Lesson Card overlay (Mira appears) → dismiss → fade back to **Desk**
3. **Level up** → gold particle burst on the desk surface, Mira gives a thumbs up

Total transitions: 4 simple Framer Motion animations. Cheap, high polish.

## Art generation pipeline (to execute at H18–H22)

1. Generate 1 reference image first (e.g. the Desk) and lock the style
2. Feed that as a reference for all 8 scene backgrounds (Nanobanana session consistency)
3. Generate 8 scene backgrounds
4. Generate 27 portraits (using one portrait as reference for consistency)
5. Generate Desk hub image + variations for time-of-day if we have buffer
6. Curate — regenerate any that break style
7. Lightly post-process in Photoshop/Figma if needed (color match, crop)

Total human time: ~3 hours if Nanobanana is behaving, with the rate limit already burned.

**Backup plan**: Higgsfield Soul for stills if Nanobanana hits quota again.

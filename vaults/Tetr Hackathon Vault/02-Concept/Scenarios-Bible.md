# Scenarios Bible — CLUTCH

All playable scenarios across 6 districts. Launch target: **12 scenarios at H22** (2 per district, enough for a full playable demo + free/Pro split). Content drops: **+12 more by H48** (2 Act 2 scenarios per district).

Format per scenario:
- **ID · Title · Tier** (Free / Pro)
- **Scene** (where it happens, 1 sentence)
- **Counterpart** (archetype + portrait idea)
- **Your goal** (explicit anchor + walk-away)
- **Star rubric** (1★/2★/3★)
- **Teaching moment**
- **Unlockable tactic** (if any)

## 💕 DATE DISTRICT — "Uptown Café"

### D1 · First Date Opener · FREE (tutorial)
**Scene**: Corner booth, coffee steaming, awkward hello.
**Counterpart**: Ana — "The Skeptical Hinge Match" (warm but guarded).
**Your goal**: Earn a second date. Get her to propose or agree to one.
**Stars**: 1★ polite goodbye · 2★ she suggests seeing you again · 3★ she texts you "had fun" by scene end
**Teaching**: Callbacks work. Asking about her profile line (not just her job) signals you paid attention.
**Unlocks**: *Callback* tactic.

### D2 · The "What Are We" Talk · FREE
**Scene**: Her place, 4 weeks in, Friday night.
**Counterpart**: Maya — wants commitment but won't ask.
**Your goal**: Land on mutual understanding without scaring her off OR over-committing.
**Stars**: 1★ no breakup · 2★ defined as exclusive · 3★ she says she's happy + plans made for something a month out
**Teaching**: Vulnerability scored higher than deflection. Humor ≠ avoidance.
**Unlocks**: *Vulnerability* tactic.

### D3 · "You've Been Distant" · PRO
**Scene**: Text convo then FaceTime.
**Counterpart**: Your 6-month partner, slump period.
**Goal**: Recover intimacy without over-promising or defensiveness.
**Stars**: 1★ damage contained · 2★ plan for the weekend · 3★ she says "thanks for hearing me"
**Teaching**: "I hear you" before "but here's why" unlocks everything.

### D4 · Meeting the Friends · PRO
**Scene**: Dinner with her 3 best friends.
**Counterpart**: The friend group (one hostile, one neutral, one ally). Group dynamics.
**Goal**: Win over the hostile friend without kissing ass.
**Stars**: 1★ survive · 2★ make them laugh twice · 3★ hostile friend texts her "I approve"
**Teaching**: Find the ally first, neutralize the skeptic through respect, not charm.

### D5 · Breakup Prevention · PRO
**Scene**: She says "we need to talk."
**Counterpart**: Her, ready to end things.
**Goal**: Either save it *or* end it with grace. Both can be 3★.
**Stars**: 1★ no screaming · 2★ closure or reset · 3★ mutual respect maintained
**Teaching**: Acceptance is not defeat. Sometimes 3★ is a clean breakup.

## 💼 INTERVIEW DISTRICT — "Cubicle Corners"

### I1 · "Tell Me About Yourself" · FREE (tutorial)
**Scene**: Zoom interview, you're on camera.
**Counterpart**: Dan — mid-level engineering manager, tired of generic answers.
**Goal**: 90-second answer that sets up the rest of the interview.
**Stars**: 1★ he doesn't zone out · 2★ he writes a note · 3★ he asks a follow-up about a specific thing you said
**Teaching**: Lead with the through-line, not the chronology. Drop a hook in sentence 2.
**Unlocks**: *STAR Story* tactic.

### I2 · "Why You Over 3 Others?" · FREE
**Scene**: Final round, CEO in the room.
**Counterpart**: Ioana — blunt CEO, decision-maker.
**Goal**: Don't brag. Don't whine. Differentiate.
**Stars**: 1★ stay in contention · 2★ she nods · 3★ she says "we'll be in touch" in a real way
**Teaching**: Contrast > superlatives. "I bring X where most candidates bring Y" >>> "I'm the best."

### I3 · Behavioral: Tell Me About a Failure · PRO
**Scene**: Sr. interviewer, poker face.
**Counterpart**: Marcus — looking for humility + learning + no blame.
**Goal**: Pick the right story. Land it in 2 minutes.
**Stars**: rubric-based on STAR method completeness
**Teaching**: The failure you pick is the message. Don't pick one where you dodged fault.
**Unlocks**: *Reframe* tactic.

### I4 · Salary Expectations Landmine · PRO
**Scene**: Final round, end of interview.
**Counterpart**: Diana — HR director with a band.
**Goal**: Anchor high without getting screened out. Navigate "what are you currently making?"
**Stars**: 1★ offer extended · 2★ offer above midpoint · 3★ offer at or above top-of-band with signing bonus
**Teaching**: Ranges > numbers. Tie to market research, not personal need.

### I5 · The Offer Negotiation · PRO
**Scene**: Offer email received, reply call scheduled.
**Counterpart**: Diana again, now with the offer in hand.
**Goal**: Improve the offer on 2 axes (base + vesting OR bonus + vacation).
**Stars**: 1★ get anything extra · 2★ one axis improved significantly · 3★ two axes improved
**Teaching**: Never negotiate one thing. Bundle the ask.

## 🤝 DEAL DISTRICT — "The Marketplace"

### DE1 · Facebook Marketplace iPhone · FREE (tutorial)
**Scene**: Parking lot, your old iPhone 13 for sale.
**Counterpart**: Marius — your "cousin" (or someone like him).
**Goal**: Sell at/near asking (€450). Walk-away €380.
**Stars**: 1★ sell at any price · 2★ sell at ≥€420 · 3★ sell at ≥€440 with no condition lies
**Teaching**: First-to-speak-a-number often loses. Let them ask for a discount.
**Unlocks**: *Anchor* tactic.

### DE2 · Used Car Haggle · FREE
**Scene**: Car lot, Vasile's office.
**Counterpart**: Vasile — The Old-School Dealer.
**Goal**: Buy the €18,000 BMW under €16,500.
**Stars**: 1★ any deal · 2★ ≤ €16,000 · 3★ ≤ €15,700 + free service included
**Teaching**: Silence is the strongest tactic. Mirror his phrasing back to extract info.
**Unlocks**: *Silence*, *Mirror*.

### DE3 · Freelance Pricing Pushback · PRO
**Scene**: Zoom call, client proposing 40% below your rate.
**Counterpart**: Razvan — The Cheap Founder.
**Goal**: Close at or above your rate, OR walk away clean.
**Stars**: 1★ close or clean walk · 2★ full rate · 3★ full rate + 50% upfront + milestone structure
**Teaching**: Tie price to *their* outcome (revenue, hires saved) not your hours.

### DE4 · Enterprise Sales Call · PRO
**Scene**: First call with a €250k/year account.
**Counterpart**: Svetlana — Corporate Procurement Bot.
**Goal**: Get to pilot stage without discounting the sticker.
**Stars**: 1★ next-meeting booked · 2★ pilot agreed at full price · 3★ pilot agreed + intro to 2 more stakeholders
**Teaching**: Procurement doesn't care. Find the economic buyer and include them in the next call.

### DE5 · Saving a Cancelling Customer · PRO
**Scene**: Customer emails "we're not renewing." You have 1 call to change that.
**Counterpart**: The VP Who's Been Burned (Ioana).
**Goal**: Either save the renewal OR get a clean NPS referral if leaving.
**Stars**: depend on which path + how it ends
**Teaching**: The reason behind the reason. First "why" is rarely true — second "why" is.

## 🎤 STAGE DISTRICT — "The Spotlight"

### S1 · Startup Competition Q&A · FREE
**Scene**: Pitch just done, 3 judges asking hard questions.
**Counterpart**: Panel of 3 — skeptic, domain expert, finance hawk.
**Goal**: Handle 3 questions without bombing any.
**Stars**: 1★ survive · 2★ 2 strong answers · 3★ one judge offers a follow-up meeting
**Teaching**: Reframe > defend. Concede the small point, win the big one.
**Unlocks**: *Reframe*.

### S2 · Hostile Debate · PRO
**Scene**: Panel discussion, your opponent is attacking.
**Counterpart**: Loud antagonist, crowd of 80.
**Goal**: Win the room without losing your composure.
**Stars**: 1★ hold ground · 2★ reframe the terms · 3★ audience laughs with you at least once
**Teaching**: Never attack back — attack the *position*, concede to the *person*.

### S3 · Press Conference Damage Control · PRO
**Scene**: Your startup shipped a bug. Press is asking why users lost data.
**Counterpart**: 3 journalists, 1 hostile.
**Goal**: Honest, no-spin, protect the company.
**Stars**: rubric on honesty + specifics + no-blame-game
**Teaching**: Concrete timeline + explicit ownership + next-action. Anything else is damage.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 FAMILY DISTRICT — "Home Turf"

### F1 · "When Are You Settling Down?" · FREE
**Scene**: Sunday lunch at parents'.
**Counterpart**: Mama — loving but relentless.
**Goal**: Redirect without being cold.
**Stars**: 1★ survive · 2★ subject changes naturally · 3★ she hugs you after
**Teaching**: Acknowledge before redirect. "I know you ask because you love me…"
**Unlocks**: *Acknowledge*.

### F2 · Asking to Borrow Money · PRO
**Scene**: Coffee with your older sibling.
**Counterpart**: Your sister, financially stable, wary.
**Goal**: Get the loan with dignity.
**Stars**: 1★ maybe · 2★ yes with structure · 3★ yes + she offers to help beyond the money
**Teaching**: Frame = investment not favor. Specifics = trust.

### F3 · Saying No to an Expensive Wedding · PRO
**Scene**: Your close friend's destination wedding. €1,800 you don't have.
**Counterpart**: Best friend, hurt easily.
**Goal**: No, with relationship intact.
**Stars**: 1★ no hurt · 2★ alternative offered · 3★ friendship *stronger* afterward
**Teaching**: "I can't" ≠ "I don't want to." Offer the replacement before stating the decline.

## 😬 AWKWARD DISTRICT — "The Gray Zone"

### A1 · Firing an Underperforming Friend · FREE
**Scene**: Friday conference room. Your cofounder + the friend you hired.
**Counterpart**: The friend — blindsided, about to cry.
**Goal**: Fire them + keep the friendship alive.
**Stars**: 1★ termination completes · 2★ they accept it · 3★ they thank you within 30 days (shown in epilogue)
**Teaching**: Kindness ≠ softness. Specifics > platitudes. Offer a bridge (severance / LOR).
**Unlocks**: *Firm-Kind*.

### A2 · Rent Negotiation with a PA Roommate · PRO
**Scene**: Kitchen, she's been "joking" about your habits.
**Counterpart**: Passive-aggressive roommate.
**Goal**: Fair rent adjustment + clear boundaries.
**Stars**: 1★ issue discussed · 2★ agreement reached · 3★ agreement + relationship reset
**Teaching**: Name the tension. "I notice we've been sideways — want to just talk about it?"

### A3 · Return a Used Item · PRO
**Scene**: Store customer service desk.
**Counterpart**: By-the-book clerk + line of people behind you.
**Goal**: Get the refund, no store credit.
**Stars**: 1★ store credit · 2★ full refund · 3★ full refund + apology from the clerk
**Teaching**: Policies bend to *politeness + persistence*, never to anger.

## Writing rubric (so every scenario feels authored, not generated)
- 1 specific **cultural anchor** (Romanian flea market, Hinge profile, Slack message pinged)
- 1 **micro-detail** that grounds it (the coffee going cold, the fluorescent bulb flicker)
- 1 **surprise line** counterpart can drop that catches user off-guard
- 1 **face-saving out** (always a path to 1★ so no one hard-fails and rage-quits)

## Character voice bible note
We do NOT use Chris Voss or Robert Greene as in-game coaches (overdone). The coach character will be original: **"Mira"**, late-20s, warm, direct, funny, speaks in specific examples, never in buzzwords. System prompt for Mira in `Art-and-Voice-Brief.md` (TBW).

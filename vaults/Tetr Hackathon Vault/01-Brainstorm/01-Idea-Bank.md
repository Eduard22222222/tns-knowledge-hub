# Idea Bank — 10 Concepts

Ranking axes: **Virality**, **Monetization clarity**, **48h buildability**, **Learning depth**, **Judge wow-factor**. 1-5 each.

| # | Concept | Viral | $ | Build | Learn | Wow | Total |
|---|---------|-------|---|-------|-------|-----|-------|
| 1 | **Pitch to AI Sharks** — pitch your startup idea (text/voice) to 4 AI investors with real personalities (Thiel/Buffett/Shark Tank/Andreessen). Get grilled, get a scorecard, get a shareable verdict. | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | **24** |
| 2 | **Salary Negotiation Arena** — you walk into a raise conversation with an AI boss archetype (Cheap Founder, Corporate HR, Empathetic Director). Negotiate via chat. Scored on anchor, justification, BATNA, tone. Share your $ outcome. | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | **24** |
| 3 | **Founder's Week from Hell** — 7 "days" of CEO emergencies (PR crisis, co-founder quits, term sheet expires). Make calls in 60 sec each. Consequences stack. Ends with company valuation + "why you failed/won" reel. | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 5 | **22** |
| 4 | **Cold Email Coach** — write cold emails, AI prospect responds realistically (interested / ghosts / angry / replies). Score on subject line, opener, CTA. Weekly leaderboard. | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | **19** |
| 5 | **Consulting Case Battles** — McKinsey-style case interviews with AI interviewer. Market sizing, profit tree, go/no-go. Score like real firms do. | 3 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 3 | **18** |
| 6 | **Cofounder Dating Sim** — pick co-founder, navigate 10 scenarios (equity split, hiring disagreement, acquisition offer). Relationship meter. Company survives or implodes. | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 5 | **19** |
| 7 | **Price This** — daily product drops, you pick the price, see against market + competitors, LLM explains why you were right/wrong. Streaks, leaderboard. | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 3 | **19** |
| 8 | **Build-a-Brand in 5 Minutes** — AI asks 10 rapid questions, generates your brand's TAM/moat/GTM/pricing, you defend it against AI critic. | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | **17** |
| 9 | **Unit Economics Escape Room** — puzzles where you unblock a failing SaaS by fixing CAC/LTV/churn/burn. Real frameworks hidden inside. | 2 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 3 | **16** |
| 10 | **Fire the Employee** — HR sim. Must let someone go for performance. AI employee reacts (denial, anger, bargaining). Rated on empathy + legal + business. Niche but viral on LinkedIn. | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | **19** |

## Filter
Top contenders by total: **#1 Pitch to AI Sharks**, **#2 Salary Negotiation Arena**, **#3 Founder's Week from Hell**.

All three share a winning DNA:
- LLM-as-judge (your only engine — fast to build)
- Emotional stakes (people *care* about the verdict)
- Shareable artifact (scorecard / pitch roast / valuation card)
- Clear paywall (more investors / harder opponents / unlock archetypes)
- Teaches a real business skill

See `02-Top-3-Concepts.md` for deep dives and `03-Winner-Recommendation.md` for my pick.

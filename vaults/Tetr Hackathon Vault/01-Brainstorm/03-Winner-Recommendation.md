# Winner Recommendation

## Pick: **Concept A — "Pitch Pit"** (with a strong case for a hybrid)

### Why Pitch Pit wins
1. **Viral artifact is strongest** — a "roast card" from 4 distinct personalities is more shareable than a salary number or a company valuation. Anything that makes people laugh + get competitive spreads fastest.
2. **Solo-play friction is lowest** — user enters *one line* ("Uber for X") and the game begins. Raise Me needs scenario picking. Founder's Week needs 12-minute commitment.
3. **Demo aesthetics in front of judges** — 4 animated shark panels streaming in parallel is visually dramatic. Demos well live.
4. **Monetization ladder is obvious** — more sharks, custom sharks, deck export, post-mortem — all clear upsells you don't have to invent on the spot.
5. **Content moat is cheap** — once the 4 base shark prompts are tuned, every new pitch = infinite replayability. No content treadmill.

### Why NOT pick the others
- **Raise Me** is objectively more useful in life — but "useful" doesn't win hackathons in 48h, *addictive* does. Also harder to make feel like a *game*.
- **Founder's Week** is the most ambitious and game-feeling — but content authoring at 21 events × branches in 48h is a trap. Cut if judges reward polish over ambition.

### The hybrid play (optional, if we have buffer)
Ship **Pitch Pit** as the core product. Add a "**Sharks' Week**" mode inside it (3rd-day feature drop): after 3 pitches, sharks "invest" in one, and you play a compressed 3-day Founder's Week. This gives us A's launch velocity *and* C's depth as a retention hook for premium users. Do NOT attempt on day 1.

### What we commit to in the brief
- **Problem**: People with ideas can't tell if they're good, can't practice pitching, and get destroyed in real investor meetings because no one role-plays with them.
- **Validation**: Reddit r/Entrepreneur has 4.7M members. "Roast my pitch" threads get hundreds of comments. Shark Tank is a billion-dollar IP. Pitch practice is a real pain.
- **Solution**: Pitch Pit — instant, personality-driven, shareable pitch practice with a learning scorecard.
- **Why now**: LLMs finally good enough to simulate investor archetypes convincingly.

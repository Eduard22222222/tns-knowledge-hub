# Tetr College of Business — 48-Hour Startup Hackathon

## Theme
**Design a Business Game** — interactive business simulation where users *learn business by practicing it*.

## Why this theme exists (read between the lines)
Traditional business education is passive. Tetr is betting that the next wave of learners want simulation, not slides. Judges will reward products that create **real business intuition** through decision-making, feedback loops, consequences, and progression.

## The Challenge (48h)
- Identify a real-world business skill or problem (strategy, sales, negotiation, operations, etc.)
- Build a functional game / simulation prototype (web / app / platform)
- Mechanics must enable learning-by-doing via:
  - Decision-making
  - Feedback loops
  - Consequences & outcomes
  - Progression systems

## Milestones (all four count)
1. **Identify** a business problem and validate it
2. **Build** a functional MVP using vibe-coding platforms
3. **Acquire** at least 100 users who play the game
4. **Monetize** — generate $100 revenue by making parts of the game paid

## Judging Criteria
1. Problem Relevance & Clarity
2. Product Thinking & UX
3. Quality of Simulation & Learning Experience
4. Creativity in Game Design
5. Impact on User Understanding of Business

## Sample directions Tetr surfaced
- Strategy Simulation (build & scale a business)
- Consulting Game (solve client problems, get scored)
- Sales Simulation (close deals, handle objections)

## What actually wins this hackathon
Not the most complex sim. The team that:
- Picks a **sharp, emotionally charged business moment** (negotiating salary, pitching VCs, firing a co-founder) instead of a generic "build a company" sandbox
- Has a **shareable output** per play (score card, roast, verdict) so 1 user → 5 users organically
- Nails **one** mechanic deeply (LLM judgment + consequence) instead of 10 mechanics shallowly
- Shows **real revenue** with a paywall that feels earned, not forced

## Timebox reality check
- 48h = ~16h coding, ~8h content/polish, ~16h launch/marketing/user acquisition, ~8h sleep+food
- If we code past hour 30, we lose. Ship by hour 30, launch hour 31-47.

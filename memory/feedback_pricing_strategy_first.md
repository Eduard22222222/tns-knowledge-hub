On the Clutch pitch deck (2026-06-25), Eduard pushed back: I had put a €48k MRR revenue
projection + premium €1.5–3k/mo pricing straight into the deck **without first explaining or
agreeing the pricing model and sales strategy**. His words: "you didnt tell me the pricing
model, sales strategy you just jumped to it, its not ok." He also found €48k MRR unrealistic
and set the anchor: **pricing should be competitive, ~$650/month** (not premium white-label).

**Why:** he's the founder/decision-maker on go-to-market; pricing + sales motion are his
strategic calls, not mine to assume. Numbers in a deck must follow from an agreed model, and
projections must be realistic/conservative, not aspirational.

**How to apply:** for anything revenue/pricing/GTM, first present the model (price points,
tiers, sales motion, channels) and a conservative projection as a proposal, get his
confirmation, THEN build it into the deck/deliverable. Keep projections grounded. Default to
competitive pricing (~$650/mo core) unless he says otherwise. See [[feedback-proposal-style]]
and [[project-consumervoice-leadgen]].

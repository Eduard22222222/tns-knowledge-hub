On the Auto'sWorld prototype v1 (2026-07-10), Eduard rejected the first design hard: "se vede că e vibecoded, nu-mi place absolut deloc". Specific corrections: (1) generic fonts don't cut it — display type must be EYE-CATCHING (Clash Display from Fontshare landed well in v2); (2) always use the client's REAL logo (he had asked earlier — don't substitute a text wordmark); (3) canvas-drawn hero animations read as fake — he prefers REAL cinematic stock video (Pexels "black Bentley headlights at night" 6872095 was his call and it transformed the page); (4) AI-generated (Gemini/Nanobanana) product photos were rejected even when faithful — "se vede că-s AI generated"; the ORIGINAL shop product photos are fine; if AI imagery is ever needed it must be ultra-realistic (Higgsfield + strong prompting) and preserve the diecast-model look; (5) he asks for React Bits & similar libraries for dynamic front-end elements, and for the design skills (frontend-design skill) to be loaded before designing.

**Why:** he sells premium design to clients; anything that smells like default AI output undermines the pitch.
**How to apply:** for ANY TNS showcase/landing/prototype: load the frontend-design skill first; source real brand assets (logo, product shots) and real cinematic footage (Pexels direct mp4 via videos.pexels.com/video-files/{id}/...); use characterful Fontshare faces (Clash Display etc.), not Google-default sans; keep AI image generation for backgrounds/scenes only if photorealism is guaranteed — never for product shots without explicit approval.

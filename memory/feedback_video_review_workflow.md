When Eduard reviews AI-generated **video** work (Seedance/Higgsfield hero clips), follow this or he gets angry fast:

- **Show the actual VIDEO files, never frame "filmstrips".** He blew up at a 3-frame strip image ("CE PULA MEA E ASTA"). To let him watch motion, copy the clips into a clearly-named folder (e.g. `ROM_HERO_VARIANTE/A_Premium_Dark.mp4`) and `Start-Process explorer.exe <folder>` so he can double-click. A static frame/strip is NOT an acceptable substitute for "show me the video".
- **When confidence is low, generate MULTIPLE themes/variants and get explicit green-light BEFORE deploying.** After several rejected hero attempts he said "mi-am pierdut increderea... incearca mai multe teme, variante... nu mai da niciun deploy, imi arati video-ul si eu ti dau green light." Offer 3-4 distinct directions, let him pick, deploy only the chosen one.
- **Stop self-praising the output.** Enthusiastic "this is photoreal / a massive leap" reads as mocking when he thinks it looks fake ("ba tu ma iei la misto"). Present plainly, give honest trade-offs, let him judge.

**Why:** he has a sharp eye for AI tells and is paying-client-conscious (these are TNS demos); being over-sold a bad result destroys trust. **How to apply:** for any AI hero-video task — render → put files in a folder → open it → let him watch → pick → only then build/deploy.
